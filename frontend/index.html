Green Turtle Trades

Green Turtle Trades is a UK trading analytics and verified trader platform operated by Green Turtle Trades Limited, company number SC878660.

People may also search for Green Turtle Traders because the official domain is greenturtletraders.co.uk. The brand name is Green Turtle Trades.

The platform helps traders review real trade history, keep a structured trading journal, use Turtle Analytics, and build transparent performance records for trader evaluation.

The public University Competition page shows Scotland's next generation of investors, ranked live through university rankings, student trader scores, win rate, return, risk, and competition activity from the Green Turtle Trades competition engine.

Key Green Turtle Trades pages include the official company overview, the investment competition, the university competition leaderboard, Turtle Analytics, the trading performance tracker, and the prop firm alternative page.

The About Green Turtle Trades page explains the company story: four university students based in Edinburgh building the UK's most transparent platform for trader-backed investing, trader analytics, and performance verification.

Green Turtle Trades is not the 1980s Turtle Trading experiment and is not related to green sea turtle wildlife trade, conservation, or animal products.
