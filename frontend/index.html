Verified trading strategies for professional investors

Green Turtle Trades Limited is an Edinburgh company developing trader verification and trade-copying technology to connect independent traders with professional investors through managed accounts. The proposed investment service is for professional clients only and is in development.

Independent traders apply with a CV, broker statements and trade history. They keep their own broker accounts. Records and strategy are reviewed before any copying arrangement; applying does not guarantee selection, a contract or investor capital.

Turtle Analytics and trading journals support historical performance and risk review. The university competition uses virtual capital; competition results are distinct from a verified live trading record and from selection for the proposed investment service.
