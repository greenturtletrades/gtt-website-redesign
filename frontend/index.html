Verified trading strategies for professional investors

Based in Edinburgh, Scotland, Green Turtle Trades is developing an alternative investment platform that connects independent trading strategies with UK professional investors. Traders continue using their approved broker accounts, while GTT verifies their trading records, filters trade instructions and calculates investor allocations for execution at our designated broker.
